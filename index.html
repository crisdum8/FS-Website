// Nonprofit. Student-run. One car a year.

Empowering students to engineer the future of e-mobility. We design, build, and race a new car every year.

KTH Formula Student is a nonprofit project. Every autumn a new cohort of students joins the team, picks up where the previous season left off, and ships a new fully-electric, driverless formula car in time for summer competition.

The project is about learning to work as a team and ship real engineering under a real calendar. We compete in Austria, Germany, and the UK, but the main deliverable is the engineer who walks out at the end of the season.

03 What we do Four verbs
01

Design.

Six technical directorates, aerodynamics, chassis, powertrain, autonomy, electronics, operations, share a calendar with hard freeze dates in November, February, April and July.

Nov · freeze
02

Manufacture.

In-house fabrication, with industry partners machining what the garage cannot. Carbon layups, battery assembly, PCB bring-up, the whole stack, student-shipped.

Winter · shop floor
03

Test.

Shakedown day is the first time the full car moves under its own power. From that moment on, every weekend is track time and every weekday is a bugfix.

Spring · shakedown
04

Compete.

Formula Student Austria and Germany, the two toughest driverless competitions on the calendar. Static events, dynamic events, scrutineering, and the hunt for a podium.

Jul–Aug · Europe
