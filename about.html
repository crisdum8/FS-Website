01 The idea In one paragraph

// In one paragraph

KTH Formula Student is a nonprofit project at KTH, run by around ninety students.

Each season we design, manufacture, test and race a fully-electric, driverless formula car. We compete at Formula Student events in Austria, Germany, and the UK.

Six directorates (aerodynamics, chassis, powertrain, autonomy, electronics, operations) work against a shared calendar with freeze dates in November, February, April, and July.

The programme has run every year since 2010. This is season sixteen.
